Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Encore desktop app accesses when you connect a YouTube, Instagram or TikTok account, how it is used, and how to remove it.

Who runs Encore

Encore is a personal tool built and run by Jack Jeffries. Contact details are at the bottom of this page.

Information Encore accesses

When you connect an account, Encore asks only for what it needs to post clips and measure how they perform:

  • YouTube (through YouTube API Services): permission to upload videos to your channel, and read-only access to your channel's basic details and your videos' statistics, such as views.
  • Instagram: your account's basic profile (username and ID), permission to publish Reels, and the insights of media Encore has posted.
  • TikTok: your basic profile (display name and open ID), and permission to upload videos to your drafts or publish them.

Encore does not access your messages, followers' personal information, contacts, payment details or location.

How the information is used

  • To upload and publish the clips you choose to the account you connected.
  • To show you how your posted clips perform and to improve the app's highlight suggestions.

Encore does not use your information for advertising, and does not sell it.

Where it is stored

All information, including the sign-in tokens issued by each platform, is stored only on the computer running Encore, in a local database. Encore has no server and sends no data to anyone other than the platform you are posting to.

Sharing

Encore does not share your information with any third party. Videos and their captions are sent only to the platform you choose to post to.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept until you disconnect the account in Encore, which deletes its tokens and stored statistics from the computer. You can also revoke Encore' access at any time from each platform; see data deletion for step-by-step instructions.

YouTube API Services

Encore uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove Encore' access to your Google account at any time from your Google account's third-party connections page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will change too.
