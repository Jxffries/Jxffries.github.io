Clips

Clips is a desktop app that turns long videos and streams into short vertical clips with captions, and posts them to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

It runs on its owner's own computer and posts only to the accounts its owner connects. It is not offered to the public.

How it works

  • Finds new videos from a list of creators the owner chooses.
  • Suggests highlight moments, which the owner reviews, trims and captions.
  • Uploads finished clips to the owner's connected YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts, with the original creator credited.
  • Reads view counts for posted clips so it can suggest better moments next time.
