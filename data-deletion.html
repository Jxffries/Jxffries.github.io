How to delete your data

Last updated 26 October 2026

Encore stores data only on the computer it runs on. There are two ways to remove it, and you can use either or both.

1. Disconnect the account in Encore

Open Encore, go to Settings, then Accounts, and choose Disconnect next to the account. This deletes that account's sign-in tokens and stored statistics from the computer straight away. Uninstalling Encore and deleting its data folder removes everything.

2. Revoke access on the platform

  • YouTube / Google: open your Google account's third-party connections, select Encore and remove access.
  • Instagram: in the Instagram app go to Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites, select Encore and remove it.
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app go to Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions, select Encore and remove access.

Ask for deletion

If you would rather ask, email encoreclip@gmail.com with the account you want removed. Any data held will be deleted within 30 days and you will get a confirmation.
